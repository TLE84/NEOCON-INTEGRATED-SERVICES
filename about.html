Our Story

NEOCON INTEGRATED SERVICES was founded in 2024 with a clear mission: to provide comprehensive, innovative, and reliable engineering, procurement, installation, construction, and commissioning solutions to the oil and gas industry and beyond.

As a newly established company, we bring fresh perspectives and cutting-edge approaches to complex challenges. Our team is dedicated to building strong relationships and earning trust through exceptional service delivery and commitment to excellence.

We specialize in Electrical and Power Systems, Instrumentation and Metering, Industrial Automation and Control, Software-as-a-Service, Telecommunications Services, and Integrated Project Management. Every project is an opportunity to demonstrate our expertise and dedication.
